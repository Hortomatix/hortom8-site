[01] capabilities

Workflows that respond to the conditions actually on the ground.

HortoM8 doesn't just schedule. It senses, defers, recovers, and rebalances. Five core behaviours run continuously, with the operations team in oversight, not in the loop.

01 / 05
Wet-weather recovery

Defers mowing until turf and soil moisture return to safe levels, then initiates a catch-up protocol with adjusted intensity, height-of-cut, and night-time windows.

02 / 05
Heatwave management

Detects extreme-heat forecasts, schedules supplemental irrigation within safe windows, delays mowing during peak temperatures, and restores normal cycles when conditions stabilise.

03 / 05
Event-aware operations

Avoids scheduled games, training, school recess, and noise-restricted hours by building these constraints into workflow logic rather than depending on manual calendar oversight.

04 / 05
Multi-asset load balancing

Reassigns mowers and irrigation zones dynamically when an asset is unavailable or a zone needs additional attention, across campuses and multi-field complexes.

05 / 05
Surface conditioning

Synthetic turf and high-use areas. Schedules grooming, debris removal, and sanitation by usage intensity. Defers in heat. Coordinates with event and training calendars.

+ / 05
Custom workflows

Site-specific behaviours definable through the operations console. Built around the way your team actually works, not a generic facilities-management template.

[03] agronomic intelligence

The consultant most institutions can't afford to keep on retainer.

HortoM8 produces site-specific, weather-aware, sensor-informed agronomic recommendations on a continuous basis. The kind of advice traditionally limited to a specialist turf consultant on a long-term engagement.

  • 01
    Seasonal guidance

    Pre-emergent herbicide timing, aeration windows, renovation periods, and fertiliser cycles. Calibrated to your sites and your climate zone, not a national average.

  • 02
    Weather-driven alerts

    Wetting-agent recommendations during drought cycles. Fungal-risk warnings during warm humid periods. Frost-protection advice triggered by overnight forecasts.

  • 03
    Sensor-driven diagnostics

    Identification of hydrophobic soils, salinity issues, uneven irrigation distribution, chronic dry zones, and stress patterns, before they show up visually.

  • 04
    Regional pest and disease alerts

    Localised grub, beetle, and armyworm activity. Biosecurity notifications. Turf-disease risk modelling based on current environmental conditions.

  • 05
    Closed-loop procurement via Foliar

    When a recommendation calls for a specific wetting agent, mulch, fertiliser, or replacement part, you order it directly from pre-matched suppliers on Foliar, without leaving HortoM8.

[04] accountability & compliance

Every action logged. Every outcome attributable.

HortoM8 functions as the accountability framework between institutional owners and operators: in-house teams, specialist contractors, or hybrid arrangements. Maintenance SLAs, asset uptime, resource use, and contracted outcomes are all tracked, timestamped, and exportable for governance and sustainability reporting.
